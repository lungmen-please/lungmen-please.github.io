# LUNGMEN, PLEASE · 龙门，请出示证件

[中文](README.md) | **English**

An *Arknights* fan game in the style of *Papers, Please*.
**Based on [IP, PLEASE](https://ip-please.github.io).** Play online: <https://chenziqiadam.github.io/lungmen-please/>. This is a non-commercial fan work and is not affiliated with Hypergryph.

Around the fall of Chernobog, you work as an inspector at the Lungmen border checkpoint. The job is one thing: check the papers and decide who gets into Lungmen.
The ID states a race. The traveler has ears, horns or a halo on their head, and maybe a tail behind them. The two have to match.

## How to play

Call the next traveler → they hand over their papers → check them against the manual → stamp APPROVE or DENY (or DETAIN).

- **INSPECT**: turn on inspect mode and click two pieces of information (say, "Race" on the ID and the traveler, or "Exp." and today's date). If they contradict each other you can **Question** the traveler.
- **HAT OFF**: hats and hoods hide ears and horns. If you cannot see, ask the traveler to take it off.
- **SCAN** (from day 3): shows Originium crystals on the body, smuggled Originium, and Reunion masks.
- Each correct decision pays 5 LMD. Find the discrepancy with INSPECT before you deny or detain and you get 2 LMD more. The first two mistakes each day are warnings; after that, every mistake is a fine.
- After work you pay rent, buy food, and... Xiaoya's suppressants. Skip food and you are late the next day. Miss one dose and she gets worse; miss a second and she is taken away.
- **Records**: the Records page on the title screen keeps the endings, achievements and familiar faces you have unlocked. It survives finishing the game.
- **Language**: Chinese and English. Use the EN / 中文 button at the top right at any time. The first visit follows your browser language; you can also add `?lang=en` or `?lang=zh` to the URL.
- **Shortcuts** (desktop): Space next, 1 approve, 2 deny, 3 detain, E inspect, R scan, F manual, H hat off, Q question.

### Race guide (page 2 of the in-game manual has pictures)

| Race | Features |
|---|---|
| Feline | Triangular cat ears, a long thin tail |
| Cautus | Long rabbit ears, no visible tail |
| Perro | Droopy dog ears at the sides, a short tail |
| Lupo | Large pointed wolf ears, a bushy medium tail |
| Vulpo | Huge fox ears, an enormous tail with a white tip |
| Ursus | Round bear ears on top of the head, no tail |
| Zalak | Small pointed ears with a tuft, a big tail curled upward |
| Sarkaz | Dark horns curving backward, pointed ears |
| Vouivre | Pale straight horns, round human ears, a thick scaled tail |
| Sankta | A halo overhead, small wings |
| Liberi | Feathers at the sides of the head, no animal ears |

Headwear (a cat-ear headband, for example) is not a racial feature.

### Five days

| Day | New rules and tools |
|---|---|
| 1 | Morning: race only, with Superintendent Ch'en walking you through it. After Hoshiguma drops by: photo, expiry dates and entry permits |
| 2 | Health reports; no entry for the Infected |
| 3 | Sarkaz need a guarantee letter; the Originium scanner; detain smugglers |
| 4 | Detain Reunion members; Infected with a Rhodes Island operator card may enter |
| 5 | No entry for Ursus nationals |

Your choices, your family, and a note from nobody knows whom lead to different endings.

### Easter eggs

Story characters appear every day, but their place in the line shifts a little each run. Familiar faces from the Lungmen arc sometimes turn up at your window (at least one a day from day 2) and walk past on the road outside.
They do not always play by the rules, and whether you let them in can end up in the next day's paper. There are also small things hidden on the title screen, the date, and your desk.

## Run locally

A plain static site that loads nothing from outside. From the repository root:

```bash
python -m http.server
```

Then open <http://localhost:8000>. Double-clicking `index.html` works too.

## Editing text and translations

All text is written in Chinese in the `.js` files. At display time `i18n.js` looks it up in `lang-en.js`:

- For a whole sentence, add `'中文原文': 'English'` to `lang-en.js` (text with line breaks is split into one entry per line).
- For sentences containing numbers or names, add a regex rule at the end of that file.
- Then run `node tools/i18n_check.js`. Anything without English is listed; in the English version it would show up in Chinese.

English names for random travelers live in `I18N.names` in `lang-en.js`.

## Rebuilding the font

After changing in-game text so that it uses new Chinese characters, rebuild the subset:

```bash
pip install fonttools brotli
python tools/subset_font.py
```

The full fonts are in `tools/fonts/` (from [Fusion Pixel Font](https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font), 12px, zh_hans). The script scans every character used in the `.js`, `.html` and `.css` files.
